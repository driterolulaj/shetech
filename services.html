Services

We start with the problem, then pick the technology.

You're not buying "AI". You're buying hours back, fewer mistakes and revenue that doesn't slip away. Here's what that looks like in practice.

AI solutions

For when your team spends hours reading, sorting or summarising documents and messages.

  • Contracts, invoices and forms read by hand and re-typed into spreadsheets
  • Inboxes and support tickets that need triaging before anyone can act
  • Knowledge buried in PDFs that nobody can find quickly
  • Decisions made on gut feel because the data is too slow to pull

Every AI system we build keeps a human review step wherever it matters. Your team stays in control.

Automation

For when the same steps get repeated every day, week or month, and someone occasionally forgets one.

  • Data copied between CRM, accounting and project tools
  • Invoices, reminders and reports that depend on someone remembering
  • Onboarding and handoffs that live in one person's head
  • Errors from manual entry that cost money to fix

Custom software

For when you've outgrown spreadsheets, or no off-the-shelf tool fits the way you actually work.

  • Internal tools and dashboards built around your process
  • Customer-facing portals and B2B SaaS products
  • Integrations between systems that don't talk to each other
  • Turning a working prototype into a reliable product
