Nia: contract-to-invoice in minutes, not hours

An AI-powered platform that reads B2B contracts and sets up the entire invoicing schedule automatically, with the finance team confirming every detail.

  • AI
  • Document processing
  • Automation
  • B2B SaaS

The challenge

B2B companies manage dozens of contracts, each with its own pricing, start and end dates, and billing terms.

Finance teams were reading every contract by hand, copying the details into spreadsheets, and building invoices manually. It was slow and repetitive, and invoices were sometimes missed or wrong. Each of those meant revenue the business had earned but didn't collect on time.

The solution

We built Nia, a platform where a business simply uploads a contract.

Nia reads it and extracts the key commercial terms: parties, duration, pricing, start and end dates, and billing frequency. Then it sets up the entire invoicing schedule automatically.

The team stays in control. Nothing is billed until a person has reviewed and confirmed the extracted terms on a single screen.

How it works

  1. Upload a contract

    PDF or Word, straight from your inbox or drive.

  2. AI extracts the key terms

    Parties, dates, pricing and billing frequency, in seconds.

  3. Your team reviews & confirms

    Everything on one screen. Edit anything before it goes live.

    Human in the loop

  4. Invoices are generated on schedule

    For the whole life of the contract, with nothing to remember.

  5. Each invoice is sent automatically

    Straight to the client, on time, every time.
